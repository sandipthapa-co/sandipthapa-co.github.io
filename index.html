Creative Process

From Concept to Creation.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Brief

    Clarify the goal, audience, deadline, and format before anything is shot or designed.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Concept

    Turn the brief into ideas, storyboards, and a clear content plan.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Production

    Organize crew, equipment, and schedule, then shoot or design to the plan.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Edit & Refine

    Edit, design, and polish the work, with revision rounds until it is right.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Delivery

    Export in the right formats and hand over on time, ready to broadcast or publish.
